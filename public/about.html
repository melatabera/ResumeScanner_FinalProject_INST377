About SkillMatch

SkillMatch helps college students and graduates understand how well their resume matches the jobs they want before they apply!

What We Are Trying To Solve

Searching for internships and entry levle jobs in this market can be both overwhelming and exhausting. Not only do we have to fight hundreds of other applicants for similar roles, but it's difficult to know which roles require which skills.

SkillMatch is designed to make this processs easier by helping students identify strengths, missing skills, and areas where their resume could be more polished!

How SkillMatch Works!

01

Upload Resume

Users upload a resume or enter their skills manually.

02

Choose a Job

Users can search for jobs or paste in a specific job description.

03

Review Feedback

SkillMatch comapres the resume to the job and shows areas for improvement.

How Did We Make SkillMatch

Skillmatch uses HTML, CSS, and Javascript for the front end. The applications will connect to a Node.js backend, which handles API requests and communicates with the database.

SkillMatch uses job search and resume analysis APIs to help users comapre their current qualifications with real job requirements.

Our Mission

Our goal is to help college students and graduates like us to get a clearer picture of where they stand as applicants and hopefully give them a little bit more confidence when it comes to applying. This is a tough battle for everyone, but with the help of SkillMatch, we can make this process a little easier.
